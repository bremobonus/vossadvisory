About

DreamInc is a small team that works closely with founders, creators, and organizations to shape an idea and ship it.

We keep teams small and communication direct. You work with the people doing the work, from the first call through launch and after.

Process

  1. Talk. A short call to understand the idea and whether we're the right fit.
  2. Plan. A written scope with timeline and cost, so there are no surprises.
  3. Make. Work in short cycles, with something real to review each week.
  4. Ship. Launch, hand over everything, and stay available for what comes next.
